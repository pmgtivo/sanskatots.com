Questions Parents Ask

From what age can my child read this book? ▾
From 2 years onwards. The language is kept simple and age-appropriate so even toddlers can follow along when read aloud. The vibrant 3D illustrations also help pre-readers absorb the lessons visually. It works beautifully as a bedtime reading ritual.
Which lessons from the Bhagavad Gita are covered? ▾
22 carefully selected teachings including Karma Yoga (focus on action, not result), managing anger, Sattvic diet, Swadharma (your own duty), Dhyana (focus and mindfulness), Viveka (learning from mistakes), courage, kindness, patience, gratitude, and more — all grounded in situations children actually face.
Is Sanskrit used in the book? ▾
No complex Sanskrit. Each teaching is presented entirely in simple English, with the Sanskrit concept name included as a label (like Karma Yoga or Dhyana) so children naturally learn the terminology without any overload.
How is this different from other Gita books for kids? ▾
Most Gita books for children are either too complex, too preachy, or filled with images kids can't relate to. This book connects every lesson to modern situations children actually face today — and every lesson has 3D illustrations that make kids want to read it again. It's not a mythology book. It's an actual life-lessons guide.
Is this suitable as a gift? ▾
Absolutely — perfect for birthdays, Namkaran ceremonies, Annaprashan, Diwali, school admission gifts, and any occasion where you want to give a child the gift of wisdom. Pairs beautifully with the SanskaTots Shloka Board Book for a complete cultural learning set.
