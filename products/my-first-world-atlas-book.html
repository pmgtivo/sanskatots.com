Questions Parents Ask

How many countries are in the book? ▾
The book covers 120+ countries across its 32 pages. Each country has its own map, flag, capital city, currency, continent and popular sport, so children can learn about the world one country at a time.
What age is this atlas suitable for? ▾
It is designed for children aged 2 and above. Younger children enjoy the colourful flags and maps with a parent, while early readers can explore capitals, currencies and sports on their own.
What details are given for each country? ▾
Every country entry shows the country map, the national flag, the capital city, the currency, the continent and a popular sport of the country.
Are the pages durable and easy to clean? ▾
Yes. The pages are 300 GSM thick and laminated, so they are sturdy and water resistant. Spills and smudges wipe clean easily.
What is the size of the book? ▾
The book is A5 size — 21 cm wide and 14.8 cm tall — with spiral binding, so it lies flat when open and is easy for small hands to hold.
