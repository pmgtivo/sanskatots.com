Questions Parents Ask

From what age is this book suitable? ▾
From 2 years onwards. The 300 GSM laminated pages are sturdy and safe for daily toddler use. The ergonomic pen is specifically designed for small hands aged 2+, and the activities are calibrated for the 2–5 age range.
What are the 5 activities in the book? ▾
(1) Kannada Alphabet Tracing — every Kannada letter with 3 vocabulary words in Kannada script, English word, and pronunciation guide; (2) Number Tracing in both Kannada and English scripts; (3) Colors recognition and naming in both languages; (4) Animals with Kannada and English names; (5) Shapes tracing with bilingual names.
Is the book truly reusable? ▾
Yes — every page is printed on 300 GSM laminated paper with a water-resistant coating. Write with the included pen, wipe clean with the soft duster, and the page resets perfectly. No ghosting, no smearing. Your child can practice the same page hundreds of times.

The box includes the 32-page Kannada Write-Wipe-Repeat book, one FREE ergonomic toddler-grip pen (the DOMS Handy-Pro marker, optimised for laminated pages), and one FREE soft duster. Everything needed to open and start immediately.

Absolutely. The bilingual format with pronunciation guides means any parent can teach alongside their child — even if your own Kannada is rusty. The pronunciation guide (like "a-ra-sa" for ಅರಸ) makes it accessible for diaspora families and urban parents alike.
