Questions Parents Ask

From what age can my child use this book? ▾
From 2 years onwards. The spiral-bound A4 format lays flat for easy Velcro play, and the simple language makes each activity accessible even to very young children.
What activities are included in this book? ▾
10 activities: Meet the Gods, Gods & Their Vahanas, Ramayana Story Sequence, Krishna's Story Sequence, Ravana's Heads, Dashavatar, Hanuman's Qualities, Famous Indian Temples, Bhagavad Gita Short Verses, and a Build Ganesha activity.
How do the Velcro stickers work? ▾
The book includes 70+ reusable Velcro stickers that children peel, place, and reuse again and again — matching Gods to their symbols and Vahanas, sequencing stories, and building Ganesha piece by piece.
Is this book reusable? ▾
Yes — every page is fully laminated (300 GSM) and spiral-bound so it lays flat and holds up to repeated play. The 70+ Velcro stickers peel off cleanly and can be reused again and again, activity after activity.
Is this suitable as a gift? ▾
Absolutely — a meaningful gift for birthdays, Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi, Namkaran, Annaprashan, and return gifts. Pairs beautifully with our Bhagavad Gita for Kids and Shlokas & Mantras Board Book.
